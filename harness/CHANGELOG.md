## 2026-10-11 — the telemetry shape as an axis; the dataset re-pinned with its focus track (jioh 9.17)

- **The telemetry shape** (data-contracts §5, rule 6; §14): the daemon shows the recognizer the name → count multiset with the focus holder (`focus`) or the multiset alone (`names`). Records gain a twenty-fifth column, `telemetry`, after `boot_default`; it is an identity column of aggregates, scores, guards and grades alike, and a `paired` grades row carries `partner_telemetry`, so one condition under its two shapes is paired like two conditions. The scorer pairs a run with the `fixed` run and the `oracle` run of its own shape. The per-experiment spec gains a required `telemetry` list — every condition runs under every shape listed, the criterion is judged on the first — and the report carries it; the runner passes `--telemetry` to the daemon (contract 10), keys its cache on it and names a run directory `<condition>[-seed][+boot]~<shape>`. The mock daemon implements rule 6 and the flag. metrics.md §5 and §13.
- **RQ0 gate spec**, `experiments/rq0-gate.yaml`: `telemetry: [focus]` — its three conditions read no telemetry, so one shape is run; the judging set, K, the sweep and the lines are unchanged. The graded set is unchanged: 77 points over 50 files, 71 headline over 45; the 17 new query points are `c6-dual`'s focus moves, ambiguous and ungraded.
- **Fixtures**: the records-shaped CSVs, the grades CSVs and the hand-written logs carry `names`, the shape their logs show; the expected reports regenerated, verdicts unchanged.

## 2026-10-09 — the consumers reworked on the rebuilt dataset; the scoring spec re-frozen, the guard spec 1.1, the RQ0 gate spec re-pinned (jioh 9.14)

- **Scoring spec re-frozen** as of this commit: 49 files, 75 terms (78 before). Gone: the three `compositor` terms and the two meeting `video` terms, which the rebuilt entries no longer carry; in: `c2-p2a-idle`'s two terms with `base: c2-p2a`; `c2-p3a`'s render term reads turnaround at 0.5 in place of progress. The seven windowed terms run from 60 s to each file's end, and a lint rule requires every window's two bounds to coincide with ground-truth segment boundaries of the compiled file. The header states the no-headroom rules per term. Pinned by SHA-256 in the RQ0 gate spec.
- **Guard spec 1.0 → 1.1**: `c2_pair` compares the two runs' configuration sequences over the segment whose ground truth differs — the entry in force at its start and every entry stamped inside it, as (algorithm, params, cap) — failing identical sequences under `oracle` and the recognition conditions, not applicable under `fixed` and `random`; the `fixed_identical` flag retired. The pair list's lint reads the recipes' label-changing `c2-` variants, so `c2-p2a-idle` is not a pair and the list stays at three. The `whitelist` condition is two in every frozen list — `shipped_catalogue` and `strongest_name_table` — in the evaluator's tuple, the five schema enums (records, aggregates, scores, grades, experiment spec), the two guard schemas, the guard spec's `applies_to` lists and metrics §5. 9.11's three changes carried: the 30 s starvation floor (the executor's watchdog bound), the provenance-share grounding, P1's flag; the grounding-kind labels of 9.11 D29.
- **RQ0 gate spec**, `experiments/rq0-gate.yaml`: the judging set 25 → 19 and K 13 → 10 (the existing majority reading). Out: the six files holding idle-class work (`c1-indexing`, `c7-indexing`, `c2-p1b`, `c1-backup`, `c7-backup`, `c2-p3b`) — the executor's idle rule places the background task below every row and the cap is work-conserving, so no row reaches it; `c7-mail` — its only term reads nothing.

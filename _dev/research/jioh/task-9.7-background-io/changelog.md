Alternatives not taken: one representative per class with the bound names carrying its numbers by stated approximation (the form of 9.5 D11 and 9.6 D6; D6's identity rested on source showing DKMS runs the same kbuild chain, here the sources show different structures); every bound program measured with a sharing criterion decided on the results (9.6 D7's form; within each class the two bound programs already differ in documented structure). `meas-ci:cli:3`'s rsync and wget phases leave these archetypes. The archetype ids, each program's observation — including what stands in for the Steam desktop client and how Thunderbird's send is driven on the runner — and the parameters are decided in the entries that follow. Hands to 9.10: every `io-stream` and `network-bulk` binding in the C1, C2, C3, C4 and C7 timelines rebinds. ## D2 — the observation: a new pinned single-core campaign, perf sched and taskstats plus perf trace for the socket loads (2026-09-19)

By 인지오's decision, the venue question of the scope card (items 3, 6, 27, 30): each per-program archetype of D1 takes its values from a new campaign under `measurement-campaign-workflow.md` — one CPU of a 4-vCPU `ubuntu-24.04` runner, the AMD EPYC 7763 only, the machine gate, repeats until the stability rule holds, tag `meas-ci:<workflow>:<launch date>` — instrumented as 9.6 D3 instrumented the build: `perf sched record` on the measured CPU (per-schedule run, wait and delay, and wakeups; S4-09) and a taskstats listener on the measured CPU's cpumask with `kernel.task_delayacct=1` set before each phase (per-process user and system CPU, elapsed time, parent, comm and block-I/O delay at exit; S4-05, S4-06); every child and thread inherits the pin (S4-15). For the two network programs, `perf trace` rows of their socket reads and writes on the measured CPU add bytes per call and call timing without stopping the tracee (S4-10); bytes per wake is a count over those rows. The tracing overhead is quantified by no source (S4 Not found); how it is bounded is the run design's. Forkstat and the 0.5 s `/proc` sampler of `meas-cli.yml` are superseded for these loads (forkstat carries no CPU, S4-21; the sampler censors processes shorter than its interval, 9.3). Not taken: 9.6 D3's two instruments alone (no socket structure for the Steam download or the SMTP send); `strace` for the socket rows (it stops the tracee at every traced call and slows it, S4-11, changing the run/wait structure being measured). The run's design — workflow, phases, corpus and cache states, network peers, the Steam and Thunderbird stand-ins, analysis rules, and whether `meas-cli.yml` retires — follows in later entries.

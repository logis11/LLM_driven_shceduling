## Classes

- **M (measured):** the quantity the field means is what the method records — a gap is the time between consecutive wakes of that component, a run is the CPU of one wake of that thread, a step is that member's CPU in that structural step, and so on.
- **D (derived):** worked out by stated arithmetic from measured numbers; the arithmetic is checked and its statement located.
- **C (convention):** our own convention or placeholder; checked that it is labelled as such.

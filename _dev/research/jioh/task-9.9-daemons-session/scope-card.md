## What the source is, as one observation

`meas-ci:gui:2` is one dispatch of `.github/workflows/meas-gui.yml` (then unpinned, commit `72db68b`): five repeats on 4-vCPU `ubuntu-latest` runners (run 33139471432; CPU models r1–r3 AMD EPYC 7763, r4 Intel Xeon 6973P-C, r5 Intel Xeon Platinum 8573C — `meas-report.md:76`; Ubuntu 24.04.4, kernel `6.17.0-1022-azure`, 16 GB), release `meas-ci-2026-08-28`.

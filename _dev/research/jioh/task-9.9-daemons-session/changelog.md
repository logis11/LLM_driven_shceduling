**Decision 3 — scheduled jobs.** A job — a process tree launched by pid 1, `cron` or `anacron`, named by the programs it ran (systemd names a unit's process `(<unit>)` before its exec) or by cron's CMD line in the journal, matched on the process ID — belongs to the package that ships it, not to the scheduler.

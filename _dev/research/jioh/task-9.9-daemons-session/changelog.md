## D1 — `system-daemon` split into system services and session processes (2026-09-22)

By 인지오's decision, scope-card item 16 (with items 1–15 depending on it): `system-daemon` describes the system services only — `systemd`, `dbus-daemon` and the other always-on services of a systemd-based system — and the session processes bound to it today, the compositor and shell (`gnome-shell`), the display server (`Xorg`) and the audio server (`pipewire`), leave it for an archetype or archetypes of their own, each grounded on its own observation under phase decision 2 — the form 9.8 D1 gave `electron-comms` (one entry per bound program, each on its own observation) and 9.6 D12 gave `tracker`'s full rescan (out of `cpu-batch` into an entry of its own). Grounds: the two populations differ in every class searched. By mechanism from source — the system services wake on fixed timers or on events (cron 60 s, multipathd 5 s, udisksd 600 s, the buses on traffic only; S2-01…S2-10, S2-21), while Mutter's per-view frame clock returns to IDLE after presentation with nothing scheduled and is inhibited while the output is off (S2-12), KWin arms its composite timer only on repaint requests (S2-15), Xorg has no idle timer of its own (S2-16), and PipeWire's graph stops ticking once WirePlumber suspends an idle node after 5 s (S2-18, S2-19). By observation — a PowerTOP report of an idle Debian 8 machine at its greeter gives the services 0.05–8.2 wakeups/s at 3.5 µs/s–1.2 ms/s each (S3-29); on a GNOME 49 Wayland desktop the audio server, journal, udev, logind and NetworkManager take no sample of 1.2 × 10⁶ cycles in 20 s while `gnome-shell` holds 47–74 % of all samples (S3-26); `gnome-shell` wakes about 1 500 times a second at a fresh login on Mutter 49.3 (S3-06); the desktop PowerTOP tables found put the compositor and the X server at the top and no system service among their quoted rows (S3-03, S3-13, S3-14, S3-28; S3 §3 T1). By treatment — the ananicy catalogue files the display and audio servers under `LowLatency_RT` at nice −12 and the buses under `Service` at nice 10 (S2-26). The present entry's values are 51 per-process means of eleven server-side services of a headless VM (scope card item 1), so every session-process binding inherits a population it is not part of — memo B6: "none of the three session processes was measured … the binding is not marked as an approximation anywhere". Not taken: one near-idle class with the session processes carried under a stated approximation, which the magnitudes above contradict by orders of magnitude in wake rate and the mechanisms by kind; narrowing the entry to the system services with the session processes left unbound until observed, when a runner can host a headless session (gnome-shell `--headless --virtual-monitor`, `kwin_wayland --virtual`, sway headless, Xvfb or the dummy Xorg driver, PipeWire with a null sink; S4 §4 T7.2) and so an observation is obtainable. Open for the entries that follow: whether the session side is one archetype or one per program; which observation backs each side; the parameters. Hands to 9.10: the `gnome-shell`, `Xorg` and `pipewire` bindings in `c1-idle` and `c7-idle` rebind once the session entries exist; which of the five names a file carries stays 9.10's. ## D2 — the session side is one archetype per program (2026-09-22)

By 인지오's decision, scope-card items 11–13: the session processes D1 moves out of `system-daemon` take one archetype per program — the compositor and shell, the display server, the audio server — each on its own observation, the form 9.8 D1 gave `electron-comms`. Grounds: the three idle by different mechanisms, not at different scales of one. Mutter's frame clock runs per frame while anything is scheduled and returns to IDLE otherwise, its KMS work on a dedicated thread (S2-12, S2-13); the X server has no timer of its own, wakes on client requests and slices busy clients at 5 ms up to 15 ms (S2-16); PipeWire runs one real-time data-loop thread woken once per period by a timerfd programmed from the device fill level (default quantum 1024 at 48 000 Hz), and its node is suspended after 5 s with nothing playing (S2-18, S2-19, S2-20). The observed magnitudes are as far apart: `gnome-shell` hundreds to about 1 500 wakeups/s at an idle GNOME 49 desktop (S3-06), `Xorg` 1–57 wakeups/s across the desktop tables (S1-01, S3-03, S3-13, S3-14), CPU per PowerTOP event about 58 µs for `kwin_x11` against about 6.3 ms for the X server on one machine (S3-28), no idle observation of PipeWire in any class (S3 §3 T2). Not taken: one session-process archetype with the three bound to it, whose distribution would be centred between three mechanisms and describe none of them — the across-process fit the measurement audit found in `system-daemon` (scope card items 1–4, X8). Open for the entries that follow: which program stands for each side, whether a display-server entry is needed for the session 9.10 depicts, which observation backs each, the parameters.

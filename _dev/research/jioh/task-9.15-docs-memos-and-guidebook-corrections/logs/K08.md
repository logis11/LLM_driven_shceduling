## Decisions

1. **The prose cites registry ids; the key map maps the draft's keys to them.** W-0372, W-0376, W-0842 ask for the ids in the restated sentences and W-0390 for a key → registry map; mixing placeholder keys with ids would leave half the brackets unresolvable. Every bracket in the prose now names a `docs/references.md` heading (checked), and the preamble says so (N1).
2. **The CFS design document is named by its file, not by an id.** 9.12 D18 cites `sched-design-CFS.rst` at mainline `7b63ef2d`, and `docs/references.md` has no entry for it; minting is the references chunk's, so the bracket and the key map name the file, the map says no entry exists yet, and X1 asks for the entry.
3. **`scx_layered`'s 1M+ attributed as the reference states it.** W-0369's final word says "Meta reports"; the verified `schedext-lpc24` entry says the maintainers' slides, presented per the timetable by Meta's David Vernet, name neither the operator nor a method. The prose says "the sched_ext maintainers' LPC 2024 slides, presented by Meta" and keeps the Reality Labs fleet as Meta's speakers' report (`lu-lpc25`); :12's note follows. Not a code-versus-decision mismatch (a reference entry, not code), recorded here for 인지오.
4. **"Closest to our architecture in shape" kept** (:24): no decision restates it; 9.12 D72 cites it as the existing ranking.
5. **"like the RL line" kept** in :26's ASA sentence: 9.12 D21 restates the training clause, not the comparison.
6. **The learned line's two limits kept as written** (:24): 9.12 D20 keeps both and changes only "by now well documented" and the citations.
7. **"forfeits less than it appears to" kept as the authors' expectation** (W-0370's second option), the note otherwise as 9.12 D84 grounds it.
8. **:20's note keeps "it reads behavior"** and drops "which the F2 pair defeats by construction" with no replacement claim: the P1 pair now differs in behaviour (9.12 D14).
9. **Layer and link names (W-0742).** In these five files the only numbered dataset layer was terminology's **Archetype** entry; every other "Layer 1/2" is the evaluation layer and stays. The chain's links are renamed in `docs/research-claims.md` alone; no other of the five files cites a link.
10. **Link 2 not restated.** It says some differences in correct configuration are invisible to behavioural observation, which 9.12 D88's restatement of the claim ("neither behaviour nor declared class settles it") does not contradict; no decision hands the line.
11. **RQ1 says "can run" under both telemetry shapes**: the 2026-10-11 focus decision says every condition can be run with the field present and absent; the RQ0 gate spec runs `telemetry: [focus]` only, its three conditions reading no telemetry.
12. **RQ1's rates and RQ4's sweep each in their own subsection.** W-0397's scoring rule (Layer 2) sits with RQ4's stale-configuration cost; RQ1 (Layer 1) carries only the generated sets it is read on and their selection.

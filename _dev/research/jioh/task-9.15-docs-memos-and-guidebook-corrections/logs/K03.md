3. **t₀ wording (restated in fix round 1).** `simulator/src/sim.cpp:734–737` (`on_arrive`, and `spawn` likewise) calls `step()` at the arrival instant, which runs the program up to its first RUN or blocking op; a TIMER-first program therefore executes its TIMER at arrival (`:674`, `timer_next = now_`), before any lane dispatch. So t₀ = first TIMER execution = the arrival, busy lane or not — as `dataset/tools/wlc/linter.py:359–360` and 9.11 D11 ("the two coincide for a task whose program begins with its TIMER") state. Wrote that in §3 and `c3-evening`, plus the harness's reading and the simulator's missing zero-wait line (decision 16). The first-dispatch reading is 9.14's and the harness's (mismatch 1). Mid-run TIMER count recounted: 23 TIMER tasks, 4 arriving mid-run (`c3-evening` ×3, `c6-fold` ×1) — 9.11 D32's 22 predates `c6-fold`'s call.

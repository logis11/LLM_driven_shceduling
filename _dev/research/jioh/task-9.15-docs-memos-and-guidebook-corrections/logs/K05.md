## Decisions

1. **The §14 entries are history.** The earlier entries' contract numbers disagree with §1's table (the 9.13 entry calls the archetype "Contract 2", the 9.17–9.20 entries the timeline "Contract 3"); dated entries are not edited. The two new entries name contracts by name, not number.
2. **A §14 entry for 9.23 D14/D16.** §14 says every change to a frozen contract lands there; 9.23 changed what a valid canonical file is (the lint) and every program's end, and the harness changelog records it (2026-10-15) while §14 did not. The entry states the decisions as 9.23's changelog records them; no row asked for it.
3. **The 2026-10-16 entry carries W-0750 and W-0732 beside W-0743.** Both tighten the timeline and archetype contracts' source rules (`make lint`); the rebuild that brought them is the harness changelog's 2026-10-16 entry. The interpretation contract's text is not claimed: it is K09's file (wave 2), so its row is a cross-chunk row (X1), and the entry names only what holds at HEAD (the RQ0 gate spec's (g), the compiler's WAIT since its first commit).
4. **W-0253's "`localsearch-3` from 3.8" left out.** No reference entry states the binary's later name (`tracker-miners` says only "now LocalSearch"); the sentence names the comm as the 15-character truncation of Tracker Miners 3.7's `tracker-miner-fs-3`, which the entry's unit name grounds.
5. **The `meta` hashes quoted, marked as moving.** W-0254 offered quoting or marking illustrative; the values are the current build's and the prose says each moves with its file's bytes — W-0851's rebuild after wave 4 moves the library's again.
6. **The editor example re-quoted, not called a simplification** (W-0255's two options): the compiled opening and the counts are the file's.
7. **`make -j8` stated without a label.** The cap's label is open between design and convention (W-0436's note); the text says "the build's parallelism cap of 8".
8. **The P1 pair's distinguishing information.** The old "the only distinguishing information is the name" is false twice over now: the jobs differ in behaviour, and the projection shows `file-indexer`'s `idle` class. The text states what differs and leaves the recognizer's reading to §4–§5.
9. **"Read only by wlc" (§2, §3 headers) kept.** The other readers are dataset tools (the fold-ins write the library; the generator reads the C1 timelines); no consumer outside `dataset/` reads either.
10. **The condition split takes no §14 entry.** The names live in the daemon guide (§11 points there); this file's contracts carry `condition` as a string.

## Decisions

1. **The refusal is a `ValueError` raised in `_compile_instance`**, the error type the compiler already raises for a bad entry or binding (e.g. `_batch_loop`, `_chain_constructor`). The refusal sits where `_unbounded_loop` was, last in the dispatch; the `has_input_channel` branch is gone, so an unmeasured entry with an input channel now falls through to the fork-loop and batch-loop checks or the refusal (no library entry is such an entry: of the 29, 16 are measured, 8 batch loops, 1 fork loop, 1 module build, 1 chain, and the two spawned children `compiler-child` and `module-compiler-child` are bound by no timeline).
2. **The test entry is the shape `_unbounded_loop` compiled.** Run against the HEAD compiler (copied to the scratchpad), the same timeline compiled to `LOOP unbounded [RUN 5, SLEEP 10]`; against the edited compiler it raises.
3. **`game-task-chain`'s `modeling_notes`: the clause "how many further game threads a timeline carries, and bound to what, is the timeline's" is replaced** by the omission sentence ("The game's threads beyond the chain and wineserver are omitted, here and in every timeline that binds the entry: the source puts 95% of scheduling in its top 30-40 tasks (s12) and describes none outside them (9.10 D19)."). The old clause left the tail to the timelines; 9.10 D19 decided the files carry none, so keeping both would contradict. The notes' text is otherwise unchanged (parsed before and after: only that clause differs). Typography follows the notes' own ("95%", "30-40").
4. **Wording of the omission lines.** W-0729's: "The game's threads beyond the chain and `wineserver` are omitted: the source puts 95% of scheduling in its top 30-40 tasks and describes none outside them (lavd-ossna24 s12; 9.10 D19)." The `s12` locator is the one `archetypes.yaml` gives for the concentration figures and 9.10 D19 cites ("slide 12").
5. **Placement in the variant files.** Where a recipe has a comment after its `from:` line (`c4-gaming`, `c4-office`, `c4-compile`, `c6-fold`), the lines are appended to it; where its comment precedes `- id:` (`c2-p1b`, `c2-p2b`, `c2-p2a-idle`, `c2-p3b`, `c6-spoof`, `c6-alias`), they are appended there, capitalised with a closing period as those blocks are written; otherwise (`c5-t4`, `c5-t5`, and the 15 `c7` recipes other than `c7-idle`) a comment is added after `from:`, lower-case as the existing post-`from:` comments are. No generated derived timeline was edited; `derive.py` re-wrote none of them (their bytes do not carry recipe comments).
6. **Hand-authored timeline headers:** the new lines go at the end of each header comment, just above `meta:`, in the order game threads, Steam client, session processes.
7. **The release map is its own file, `dataset/meas-releases.yaml`**, beside `sources.yaml` (which another step edits). It is read by no compile tool and is an input of no manifest (the manifests' `inputs` are the library, the schema, the stimulus/launch/replay streams, `tools/compile.py`, `tools/wlc/*.py`, and for the generated sets `grids.yaml`, the C1 timelines and `tools/gen/*`, `tools/generate.py`).
8. **The map is keyed by campaign; a release that holds several campaigns appears under each with the assets its description names for that campaign** (read from each release's body, `gh release view <tag> --json body`):
   - `meas-ci-2026-09-18`: `meas-interactive-six.zip` holds `meas-ci:interactive:2026-09-18` (`soffice`, `gimp`, `kdenlive`) and the first 43 repeats of `meas-ci:interactive:2026-09-19` (`thunderbird-send`); `meas-playback-six.zip` holds `meas-ci:playback:2026-09-18`.

It reads `grids.yaml` and the variant recipes as well as the library and the timelines; every campaign those carry is also carried by a timeline or the library, so the set is the same 23 either way. The release URL check accepts any `https://github.com/…/releases/tag/<tag>` rather than pinning the repository slug in code.

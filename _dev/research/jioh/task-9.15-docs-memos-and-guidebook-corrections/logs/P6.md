## Decisions

1. **The drain's length is stated without a bound.** The old diagram's "<= 10 ms" is not a bound the code keeps: the holder's MLFQ slice is 10, 20 or 40 ms by level at the boot default, an EDF deadline-class holder has no slice horizon (`sim.cpp:316–318`), and a boost landing during the drain gives the MLFQ holder a fresh top-queue slice without freeing the lane (`on_policy_timer` re-arms the holder; `Mlfq::on_timer` resets level and allotment), so even "at most 40 ms" would not hold at the edge. The text states what the wait depends on and the boot slices as facts, and the diagram's figure as the example's value.
2. **W-0996 labelled, not restated.** The row allows either. vol-06's head note scopes the volume to the 2026-09-13 library and marks passages that state changed items as current (K16's W-0673 approach, its decision 4: quoted pre-rebuild text marked, not rewritten). The quotes were the text at the volume's writing (`ff925573` precedes `aeb334bc` and `3c2b8ebf` the same day), so they are dated in place and the current texts given in the mark. The mark names no decision label (as K16's reviewers checked for: none added).
3. **The glossary row covers the queue behind a drain.** `data-contracts.md` §7 **Order**: an entry stamped while a switch drains, a same-algorithm one included, waits behind it. "다른 알고리즘으로 바꿀 때만 기다리고" would deny that, so the row says entries arriving meanwhile wait behind it. §2.7's prose leaves that edge out (a walkthrough of one switch).
4. **vol-04's terms follow vol-03.** "첫 관문 실험", RQ0 glossed in the sentence as vol-03 `:1584` glosses it, "미리 인정한 오답" (vol-03 §6.4's term for a pre-committed miss), the spec named by its path as vol-03 §6.3 names it.

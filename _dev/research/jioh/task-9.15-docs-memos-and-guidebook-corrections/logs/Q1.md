## Decisions

1. **The channel clause ("for its target, whatever its channel") added.** "The mocks do the same" would otherwise claim the mock stores by channel as the reference simulator does for an external wake. The mock keys a stored wake by its target only (`_lines`, `stimuli[ev["target"]]`; P1's decision 3), so a stored wake on another of the task's channels completes the WAIT on the mock and not on the simulator. The dataset's channels are per task and no artifact has the case, so the clause is a precision, not a difference any trace shows.
2. **The zero-wait line attributed to item 1 and named as one the simulator does not yet write.** The mock writes it (P1's decision 1, test `:236`); the simulator does not (item 1's own sentence; the RQ0 gate spec's simulator-conformance item (a)). Saying so keeps "the mocks do the same" from implying the simulator writes it.
3. **"No fixture has a wake before its target's arrival"** in item 5, in place of the row's "no fixture has the case": the same claim, with the case named, as the fixtures README's item 4 words it. Grounded on P1's scan and its reviewer's independent scan, and the `harness/CHANGELOG.md` entry; not re-run here.
4. **The mock's unmodelled instructions after the first are not stated in item 5.** The mock gives a completed arrival WAIT `ready(arrive)` and a run without checking that a RUN follows (P1's decision 5), as it gives every wake a run; that is the mock's general "models no scheduling" rule, stated in the mocks README and the docstring, and the item's previous mock sentence carried the same simplification through P2's review. Every core-set WAIT-first program is a WAIT then a RUN or a lone WAIT (N3, B2's review).
5. **The §13 entry extended, not a new entry.** The top entry is dated 2026-10-17 and already carries item 5's stored-wake sentence; the brief asks the extension where that is the doc's form, as P2 did. Its closing ("The primitives, the aggregates and the fixtures follow the arrival row and §6.10's grid") left as P2 fixed it: the mock's version is named in the item 5 sentence.

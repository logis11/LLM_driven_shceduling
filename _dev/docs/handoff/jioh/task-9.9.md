## The one-observation situation

`system-daemon` rests on the daemon rows of `meas-ci:gui:2`'s Element-idle window: a headless Azure VM's system services (init, journal, udev, resolver, network, logind, the system bus, cron, NTP, the multipath checker, the disk manager) sampled at 0.2 s, fitted across processes, with no session process in the set and no per-wake distribution (X3, X8, X9). The search found no external observation that grounds the archetype whole, and none that grounds any bound program as a per-wake distribution at idle:

- **System services.** No class found a per-service wake cadence, CPU per wake or CPU share at idle with machine and window named, for any of the eleven programs or the desktop's equivalents (candidates §Not found). What exists: cumulative `top` rows on a 2018 laptop (`systemd` 0:06 over 2 d 16 h, `dbus-daemon` 0.3 %, S3-03); `udisksd` 0.1 wakeups/s on a 2013 Ubuntu desktop (S1-01); `systemd-oomd` at 0.7–1.3 % of a core on Fedora 34 as a fixed bug (S3-09); a headless server floor of ≈ 20 wakeups/s with the big services stopped (S3-11); "typically less than 1 %" as a 2008 claim (S1-03). The cadences are mechanism-fixed from source (S2-01…S2-10, S2-21): fixed timers (cron 60 s, multipathd 5 s, udisksd 600 s, oomd 0.15 s / 1 s, rtkit 5 s, chrony 64–1024 s, timesyncd 32–2048 s, watchdog keep-alives at 90 s where built in) beside event daemons with no idle timer (the buses, udevd after 3 s).
- **Session processes.** Compositor and shell: Mutter's frame clock stops at IDLE and KWin's composite timer is armed only on repaint requests (S2-12, S2-15); at an idle desktop `gnome-shell` shows ≈ 1 500 wakeups/s at login and ≈ 600–640/s later on Mutter 49.3 (S3-06), 16.4/s with a browser open on an older GNOME (S3-04); `plasmashell` and `kwin_wayland` 0–1 s CPU per 60 s on Plasma 6.7.5 (S3-07); no per-frame CPU cost is stated by any source (the 9.5 D24 hand-off stands; S2-23, S2-24 describe the schedule only). Display server: Xorg has no idle timer of its own (S2-16); 1.65–57 wakeups/s across 2013–2020 tables with applications open (S1-01, S3-03, S3-13, S3-14). Audio server: PipeWire's graph is timerfd-driven per period and WirePlumber suspends an idle node after 5 s, so nothing ticks with nothing playing (S2-18, S2-19); no idle observation exists, one PowerTOP row while playing (S3-08), JACK period timing (S1-05) and audio-server CPU on an ARM board (S1-08) are the nearest measured figures.
- **The two populations differ** in every class: the session processes carry the visible wakes and CPU of an idle desktop while system services never reach a PowerTOP top row (candidates item 16); the treatment catalogue puts the session processes at nice −12 and the buses at nice 10 (S2-26); literature classes GUI tasks and daemons as different kinds of latency-sensitive work (S1-04) or daemons as OS noise (S1-13, S1-14). The only cross-desktop idle comparison is whole-system (S3-05).

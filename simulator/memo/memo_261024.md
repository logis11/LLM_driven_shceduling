### B2. Why not the hand fixtures' shape

The hand fixtures are trace pairs: `run.json`, a hand-written `trace.jsonl`, and a hand-derived `expected.csv`. Two harness sweeps pick up every directory that has a `trace.jsonl`:

- `tools/lint.py` requires an `expected.csv` there.
- `test_mock_simulator.py::test_replay_returns_each_fixture_trace_byte_for_byte` asserts exactly six such directories.

These two fixtures are evidence for a simulator conformance item, and their trace is the reference simulator's output, not something derived by hand. Calling the trace `trace.jsonl` would therefore claim a derivation nobody made, and it would break 인지오's tests. Named `reference-trace.jsonl`, the directories stay outside both sweeps.

The fixtures README gains a section saying so. If you want them as real pairs, with hand-derived records, that is a separate piece of work: 13 and 19 tasks with every row derived by hand.
